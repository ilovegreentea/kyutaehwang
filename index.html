01 · About

I study immunotherapy response as a dynamic, multi-compartment immune-system problem.

My work connects peripheral blood and tumor immune states to identify cellular programs, clonal behavior, and spatial organization associated with treatment response. I am especially interested in translational questions where high-dimensional genomic measurements can be distilled into biologically interpretable and clinically testable signals.

03 · Selected Work

Current manuscript

Metastatic renal cell carcinoma

Early peripheral T-cell dynamics and pretreatment intratumoral stress-adapted exhausted T cells

Longitudinal single-cell multi-omics profiling of paired tumor and peripheral blood samples from patients with metastatic renal cell carcinoma treated with immune checkpoint inhibitor–based therapy.

The study integrates scRNA-seq, scTCR-seq, independent cohort validation, and spatial transcriptomics to examine early peripheral T/NK-cell activation, an ATF3-linked stress-adapted exhausted CD8 T-cell program, clonotype dynamics, and macrophage–T-cell niches.
